LIFE AGAINST LOAD

Adjusted rating life of the selected bearing when the whole load case is scaled from 0.25x to 3x.

Horizontal: load, 0.25x to 3x of your caseVertical: life, log scale

Curve in green: life is at or above the required life.

Curve in red: life is below the required life.

Dashed teal line: required life L10h you entered.

Yellow square: your entered load (1.0x).

White dotted line: the highest load that still gives the required life.

Dotted grid: one line per decade of life. Faint green and red tint: above and below the required life.

Life falls with the cube of load, so a 2x load cuts life to one eighth.
